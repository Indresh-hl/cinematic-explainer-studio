## 2. The 3 Background Archetypes for Our Scenes

To ensure the video looks cohesive and matches your exact direction (*"simple, with a few items if required, or plain sky blue (let it be dull) or green"*):

> **The Minimalist Prop Rule:** If a scene requires props (e.g. the 2:14 AM alarm clock, a bed, or a retro slide projector), the props must be **isolated 2D vector items** floating or resting with a simple contact shadow on the flat background. **Never draw a full heavy room.**
