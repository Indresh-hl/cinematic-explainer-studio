# The Animated Explainer AI Video Engine — Master Prompt V13 (Clean 2D Vector & Keyword-Driven Visual Engine)

## What Changed from V12 ("Shadow Cut Film Noir & Chiaroscuro" -> "Clean 2D Vector Explainer & Minimalist Backgrounds")

V12 locked the channel to a dark chiaroscuro Film Noir aesthetic with pitch-black voids, heavy bedroom shadows, and 3D depth subtitles. **V13 completely transforms the visual and narrative engine into a high-retention 2D animated explainer format (modeled after *Kurzgesagt*, *TED-Ed*, and top modern European/web animation), powered by keyword-driven visual storytelling and zero subtitle clutter**:

1. **Art Style Migration (Dark Film Noir Void → Clean 2D Animated Vector & Minimalist Backgrounds):**
   - Completely discarded pitch-black voids, heavy realistic room shadows, and photorealistic 3D textures.
   - Established a permanent **Clean 2D Animated Explainer** aesthetic: crisp black vector line art, flat colors, subtle single-tone cel shading, and uncluttered, calm, solid backgrounds.
   - **The Strict Background Rule:** Solid **Dull Seafoam Green (`#45a29e`)**, **Muted Dull Sky Blue (`#6ba4b8`)**, or **Warm Cream (`#f3e5d0`)** with only a soft, clean ground contact shadow. No heavy furniture, no wallpapers, no cluttered room perspectives.
2. **Locked Protagonist Identity (Sam):**
   - Protagonist is **Sam**: stylized 2D cartoon young man in his early 20s, short tousled brown hair, bold black rectangular glasses, and large, highly expressive cartoon eyes.
   - Wears casual blue attire (blue short-sleeve shirt, blue hoodie, or clean pajamas).
   - Extreme comedic and emotional acting: wide-eyed deadpan insomnia stares, sweat droplets flying in agonizing cringe, awkward hand gestures, and dropped jaws.
3. **Integration of the Open-Source Skill `script-to-image-plan`:**
   - Eliminates the amateur mistake of generating 1 generic image for an entire paragraph of voiceover.
   - Autonomously deconstructs narration into key semantic beats using functional tags: `[HOOK]`, `[METAPHOR]`, `[LIST]`, `[PAYOFF]`, `[TURN]`, `[FACT]`, `[PUNCH]`.
   - **Keyword-Driven List Splitting:** When the script enumerates multiple items (e.g. *middle school stutter*, *high-five to fist bump*, *presentation voice crack*), each item receives its own dedicated, synchronized visual hit.
   - Enforces the strict **4-Block Prompt Architecture**: `[STYLE]`, `[CHAR]`, `[SPECIAL]`, `[NEGATIVE]`.
4. **Complete Elimination of Subtitle Bars (Subtitles → Kinetic Motion Graphics & Badges):**
   - Subtitle bars, transcription captions, and karaoke word tracking are **100% eliminated**.
   - Text is treated strictly as **Director-Level Kinetic Motion Typography**: sound-synced typing, status HUD badges (`02:14 AM // SYSTEM IDLE`, `[ MEMORY ARCHIVE: UNLOCKED ]`, `114 BPM ▲`, `SURVIVAL SIMULATION`), and bold cognitive punchlines slamming onto negative space.
5. **Multi-Platform AI Generation Compatibility:**
   - Prompts are fully optimized for **Google Flow AI**, **Midjourney v6.1**, **Flux.1**, and **Leonardo AI** with explicit 16:9 aspect ratio and strict anti-clutter negative prompts.

- **Total Shot Count:** 115 – 130 clean 2D stills cut every 3.0 to 4.5 seconds (calculated via `script-to-image-plan`).
