## 3. Gaps & Mismatches Fixed

* **Previous Gap:** The script explicitly enumerated 3 separate cringe memories (*middle school sentence*, *high-five vs fist bump*, *presentation voice crack*), but previously only 1 generic waving shot was shown for 14 seconds.
  * **Fix:** Split into 3 fast, sequential visual hits (3.5 to 4s each). The high-five vs fist bump collision is immediately recognizable and relatable to every viewer!
* **Previous Gap:** *"Every single problem from your waking day has finally vanished"* had no visual anchor.
  * **Fix:** Added a visual beat showing floating stress icons (emails, task checklists) popping and dissolving like bubbles above the bed.
* **Text in Images Rule:** No text is baked into image generation. The `02:14` clock numbers, `WAVE! WAVE!`, `CRINGE!`, `114 BPM`, and `SURVIVAL SIMULATION` badges are handled as clean kinetic overlays in the video timeline.

## 4. Master Prompt Blueprint (Using Locked Style & Character)

Every prompt below enforces the 4 mandatory blocks from the skill (`[STYLE]`, `[CHAR]`, `[SPECIAL]`, `[NEGATIVE]`):

* **[STYLE]:** `2D cartoon character animation illustration, clean bold black vector outlines, flat colors with subtle single-tone cel shading, minimalist aesthetic, plain solid dull background with only a simple soft contact shadow, zero room clutter, no wallpaper, no heavy furniture, 16:9 aspect ratio`
* **[CHAR]:** `A young cartoon man with short brown hair, black rectangular glasses, large expressive cartoon eyes, wearing casual blue clothes`
* **[NEGATIVE]:** `photorealistic, 3D render, realistic textures, cluttered background, complex room perspective, wallpaper, noisy lighting, blurry, extra limbs`
