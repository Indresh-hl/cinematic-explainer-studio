## 🏛️ System Philosophy & Core Pillars

1. **The 30-Second Somatic Hook:** Never start with abstract clinical definitions. Start inside an exact, relatable, visceral micro-moment (e.g., *6:30 AM*, heavy limbs, mental negotiation, phone doomscroll reflex).
2. **Contrarian Paradigm Reframes:** Shatter common assumptions immediately (*"It's not laziness or weak willpower—it's an involuntary biological defense mechanism of the Basal Ganglia"*).
3. **Dual-Column Production Matrix:** Every syllable of narration is married 1:1 to camera moves, lighting cuts, and specific audio/SFX cues.
4. **Rapid-Paced Word-to-Image Synchronization (No Static Holds):** Never use 1 static image for an entire paragraph. Cut every **2.5 to 3.2 seconds** (150–170 images per 9-minute video) matching each action, noun, or emotional shift.
5. **Strict 3D Pixar / Disney CGI Aesthetic:** High-end stylized 3D feature animation, rich subsurface skin scattering, tactile material rendering, dramatic Electric Cyan/Gold rim lighting, and minimalist uncluttered solid studio backdrops. **NEVER generate flat 2D cartoons, doodles, or anime.**
6. **Mobile 3-Element High-Contrast Thumbnail Rule:** 1 waist-up expressive 3D Sam + 1 tactile glowing prop + max 3 bold high-contrast words (`#FFD166`). Zero murky black voids.
7. **The 80/20 Gestalt Legibility Depth Rule:** Playfair Display subtitles tuck behind 3D character cutouts for depth whenever character occlusion is $\le 20\%$. Close-ups elevate to foreground priority.
8. **Automated 3-Part Shorts Engine:** Every master video spawns 3 standalone vertical Shorts: (1) The Somatic Hook, (2) The Brain Mechanism, (3) The Tactical Antidote.

## 🎨 Master Visual Style Architecture (LOCKED)

### 1. Protagonist Profile: "Sam"
* **Style:** 3D stylized character animation, **Pixar / Disney CGI aesthetic**, Octane Render.
* **Anatomy & Face:** Young male in his early 20s, expressive sculpted facial anatomy, thick dark brown tousled hair with realistic clump physics and specular edge highlights, bold black rectangular glasses with realistic lens reflections and zero glare over pupils, large expressive hazel eyes with double catchlights.
* **Skin & Subsurface Scattering:** High subsurface skin translucency (realistic flushed red cheeks during stress/guilt, fine sweat beads on temples, ears catching warm light).
* **Wardrobe:** Casual heather-navy cotton crewneck t-shirt with soft fabric weave, grey hoodie, or clean navy knit sweater over a white collar.

### 2. Cinematography & Staging
* **Minimalist Studio Void:** Sam and props exist on a clean, uncluttered 3D plane with soft ambient contact floor shadows and zero realistic furniture clutter or wallpapers.
* **Studio Palette Tokens:**
  - **Muted Dull Sky Blue (`#6ba4b8`):** Nocturnal isolation, desk paralysis, bedroom thoughts, and ceiling stares.
  - **Solid Dull Seafoam Green (`#45a29e`):** Primary explainer plane for cognitive models, scientific studies, blackboard reframes, and props.
  - **Warm Sand / Cream (`#f3e5d0`):** Daylight memories, evolutionary psychology, and self-compassion.
  - **Obsidian Indigo Void (`#08090c` / `#0f172a`):** fMRI scanners, dramatic single-spotlight moments, and internal mental theater.
* **Lighting:** High-contrast chiaroscuro, volumetric light shafts, warm 3200K key light contrasted with cool 6500K blue screen glow or **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)** rim lighting for mobile contrast.

### 3. Tactile 3D Props & Metaphors
* Storytelling objects must be rendered with realistic tactile materials:
  - Translucent 3D glowing glass brains with fiber-optic neural circuits.
  - Matte black digital alarm clocks with emissive red LED digits.
  - Chunky retro-futuristic red industrial levers in metallic brain bunkers.
  - Rough granite boulders vs. frictionless polished glass marbles.
  - Vintage brass balance scales and classic leather-bound notebooks.
