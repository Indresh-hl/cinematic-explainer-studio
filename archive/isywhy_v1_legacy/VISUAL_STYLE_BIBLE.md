## 1. The Core 3D Pixar / Disney CGI Visual Standard

All production renders must strictly follow the visual benchmarks established in your production master files (`assets/sampled_frame_14s.jpg`, `assets/sampled_frame_38s.jpg`, and `assets/sampled_frame_73s.jpg`):

1. **Character Architecture ("Sam"):**
   * **3D Stylized Pixar / Disney CGI Sculpt:** Expressive cartoon proportions, large hazel eyes with double specular catchlights, thick sculpted dark brown hair with natural clump physics.
   * **Subsurface Skin Scattering (SSS):** Realistic skin translucency, flushed crimson cheeks during stress/awkwardness/guilt, glistening sweat beads on temples, and ears catching warm kicker light.
   * **Glasses:** Bold matte-black rectangular frames with clean anti-reflective blue sheen, never obscuring pupils.
   * **Wardrobe:** Soft fabric weave (heather-navy crewneck t-shirt, grey hoodie, or clean navy knit sweater over a white collar).

2. **Cinematography & Staging (The Minimalist Studio Void):**
   * Sam and props are placed on an **uncluttered 3D plane** with soft ambient contact floor shadows.
   * **Zero Clutter Rule:** No messy bedroom walls, no background posters, no complex room furniture perspectives. Pure studio simplicity that channels 100% of viewer attention to Sam and the psychological metaphors.
   * **Studio Palette Tokens:**
     - **Muted Dull Sky Blue (`#6ba4b8`):** Nocturnal isolation, desk paralysis, bedroom thoughts, and ceiling stares.
     - **Solid Dull Seafoam Green (`#45a29e`):** Primary explainer plane for cognitive science models, scientific studies, blackboard reframes, and props.
     - **Warm Sand / Cream (`#f3e5d0`):** Daylight memories, evolutionary psychology, and self-compassion.
     - **Obsidian Indigo Void (`#08090c` / `#0f172a`):** fMRI scanners, dramatic single-spotlight moments, and internal mental theater.

3. **Lighting & Material Tactility:**
   * **Octane Render Aesthetic:** Rich depth-of-field ($f/1.8$ to $f/2.2$ on macro close-ups), sharp 35mm optical beams, rim lighting cleanly separating character silhouettes from the background.
   * **Tactile Materials:** Translucent glowing glass human brains with fiber-optic neural circuits, matte black alarm clocks with emissive red numerals, rough granite boulders vs. frictionless polished glass marbles.
