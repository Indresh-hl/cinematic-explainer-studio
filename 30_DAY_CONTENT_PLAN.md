* **Focus:** Habit Loop Mechanics & Neurobiology of the Basal Ganglia
* **Target Upload:** Monday (Week 1, Day 1)
* **Target Duration:** 8:30 – 9:00 Minutes (~1,480 Words, 2.4 wps)
* **The 30-Second Somatic Hook:**
  - Day 7 of a new habit (e.g. gym, reading, waking up early). 6:30 AM.
  - The initial surge of "Day 1 Motivation" has completely vanished.
  - Visceral friction: Heavy limbs, mental bargaining, opening the phone.
  - The core question: *Why does your brain violently reject self-improvement after exactly seven days?*

  - It’s NOT a lack of discipline or weak willpower.
  - It is the **Neurobiological Friction of the Basal Ganglia**: The brain prioritizes glucose conservation and treats unfamiliar routines as metabolic threats until myelination locks the neural groove.
* **Core Scientific Concepts:**
  - The Basal Ganglia vs. Prefrontal Cortex Tug-of-War
  - The Dopamine Prediction Error (Wolfram Schultz)
  - Friction Theory & Implementation Intentions (Peter Gollwitzer)
  - The 2-Minute Gateway Protocol (BJ Fogg / James Clear)
